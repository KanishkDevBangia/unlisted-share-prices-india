# Unlisted share prices, IPO calendar and GMP history — India (daily, open data)

BuyUnlistedShares (formerly Unlisted Axis) is India's #1 unlisted shares platform: the unlisted and pre-IPO desk of Gayatri Financial Synergy, Faridabad, in the market since 2002.

This repository publishes, every day, the desk's own tables as CSV:

| file | rows | what |
|---|---|---|
| `data/prices-latest.csv` | 228 | one row per unlisted / pre-IPO company the desk tracks: dated indicative price (INR per share), lot size, minimum ticket, 52-week range, ISIN, market cap at the indicative price, P/E, book value, registered-office city/state, page URL |
| `data/ipo-calendar.csv` | 170 | the IPO calendar (mainboard + SME): status, dates, price band, lot, issue size, GMP, subscription by category, allotment / refund / credit / listing dates, listing price, registrar |
| `data/gmp-history.csv` | 1072 | daily average grey-market premium per IPO for the last 90 days (rolling) |

**As of 2026-09-21** (`data/meta.json` carries the exact timestamp). Updated automatically at 06:30 UTC every day after the desk's price refresh, and again after each hourly IPO refresh while an issue is open. A commit is made only when a figure changed.

## What the figures are, and are not

* Prices are **indicative, dated, over-the-counter dealer references**. They are not stock-exchange quotes, not offers to deal and not investment advice. Unlisted shares are illiquid; values move sharply and a quote can go days without changing.
* GMP (grey market premium) is an **unofficial** indication compiled by the desk from publicly posted grey-market activity; the grey market is unregulated and a premium is not a forecast of the listing price.
* Subscription figures are as captured at the time in the row; the exchanges publish the official numbers.
* Every row is generated by the desk's own jobs from the desk's own database. Nothing is typed by hand and nothing is copied from another price site.

## Live API and pages

The same data is served as JSON with CORS `*` and a one-hour cache from the site itself:

* `https://buyunlistedshares.com/api/v1/prices.json` — every company in `prices-latest.csv`
* `https://buyunlistedshares.com/api/v1/ipos.json` — the calendar
* `https://buyunlistedshares.com/api/v1/gmp-history.json` — the 90-day GMP table
* `https://buyunlistedshares.com/api/v1/company/<slug>.json` — one company with its 400-day price series, financial table, ratios and shareholding
* `https://buyunlistedshares.com/api/v1/ipo/<slug>.json` — one IPO with GMP and subscription history, registrar and timeline

Documentation: https://buyunlistedshares.com/developers. Machine-readable site index: https://buyunlistedshares.com/llms.txt and https://buyunlistedshares.com/llms-full.txt.
Each `url` column points at the page for that company or IPO.

## Columns

`prices-latest.csv`: `slug, name, sector, industry, indicative_price_inr, price_date, lot_size, min_ticket_inr, high_52w_inr, low_52w_inr, isin, market_cap_inr_cr, pe, book_value_inr, face_value_inr, status (unlisted|listed), drhp_filed, hq_city, hq_state, url`

`ipo-calendar.csv`: `slug, name, board (Mainboard|SME), status (open|upcoming|closed|listed), exchange, open_date, close_date, price_band_low_inr, price_band_high_inr, lot_size, issue_size, fresh_issue_inr_cr, ofs_inr_cr, gmp_inr, subscription_x, sub_qib_x, sub_nii_x, sub_retail_x, allotment_date, refund_date, demat_credit_date, listing_date, listing_price_inr, listing_gain_pct, registrar, url`

`gmp-history.csv`: `date, ipo_slug, ipo_name, gmp_inr_daily_avg, gmp_pct_daily_avg, price_band_high_inr, captures`

Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`; money is INR; `min_ticket_inr` is lot size × price where the desk has not set a minimum; empty cells mean the desk has no figure, never zero.
