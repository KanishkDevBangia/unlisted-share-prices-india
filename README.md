# Unlisted share prices, India — sample dataset (monthly, delayed) + IPO calendar

BuyUnlistedShares (formerly Unlisted Axis) is India's #1 unlisted shares desk: the unlisted and pre-IPO dealing desk of Gayatri Financial Synergy, Faridabad, in the market since 2002.

This repository is a **research sample** of the desk's tables, published for citation, teaching and quick analysis. It is
deliberately small and delayed: the full, current tables (every tracked company, daily prices, GMP and subscription history,
per-company financials) live on **https://buyunlistedshares.com** and behind the desk's free-key JSON API
(**https://buyunlistedshares.com/developers**).

| file | rows | what |
|---|---|---|
| `data/prices-sample.csv` | 30 | the 30 most-tracked unlisted / pre-IPO companies on the desk (its own tracking score), with the indicative price **as of 2026-09-14** (7 days before the push), lot size, minimum ticket, ISIN, status and page URL |
| `data/ipo-calendar.csv` | 170 | the IPO calendar (mainboard + SME): name, board, status, exchange, open/close dates, price band, lot, issue size, allotment and listing dates. No GMP, no subscription figures here: those are on the site and the API |

**Pushed 2026-09-21** (`data/meta.json` has the exact timestamp). Refreshed on the **1st of every month** (06:40 UTC); a commit is made
only when a figure changed. The first day's commits (`380e0a8` … `0140cb3`, 21 Sep 2026) carried the full tables for a few
hours before the desk settled on the sample policy; they are superseded and should not be cited as current.

## What the figures are, and are not

* Prices are **indicative, dated, over-the-counter dealer references**, here a week old on purpose. They are not stock-exchange quotes, not offers to deal and not investment advice. Unlisted shares are illiquid; values move sharply and a quote can go days without changing. The current dated price is free to read on each company page (`url` column).
* Every row is generated by the desk's own jobs from the desk's own database. Nothing is typed by hand and nothing is copied from another price site.

Get a key and read the field reference at https://buyunlistedshares.com/developers. Machine-readable site index: https://buyunlistedshares.com/llms.txt and https://buyunlistedshares.com/llms-full.txt.

## Columns

`prices-sample.csv`: `slug, name, sector, industry, indicative_price_inr, price_date, lot_size, min_ticket_inr, isin, status (unlisted|listed), url`

`ipo-calendar.csv`: `slug, name, board (Mainboard|SME), status (open|upcoming|closed|listed), exchange, open_date, close_date, price_band_low_inr, price_band_high_inr, lot_size, issue_size, allotment_date, listing_date, url`

Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`; money is INR; `min_ticket_inr` is lot size × price; empty cells mean the desk has no figure, never zero.
